### Can I choose weekly, biweekly, or monthly service?

Yes. Pick the cadence that fits your household, and adjust it later if your needs change. Weekly and biweekly plans maintain the most consistent condition, while monthly works for lower-traffic homes that need only periodic upkeep.

### Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Most recurring customers provide entry instructions - a code, lockbox, or hidden key - and go about their day. If you prefer to be present, especially for your first visit, that's fine. You'll get advance notice before the crew arrives either way.

### How far in advance should I book?

Book your first cleaning 3 to 5 days ahead during regular weeks. Around holidays or peak moving season, when Scottsdale, Chandler, Gilbert, and Mesa schedules fill fastest, book 1 to 2 weeks out. Recurring customers get priority for their preferred day and time once on a regular schedule.

## What's Included in a Standard Cleaning

### What does a standard recurring cleaning cover?

A standard cleaning covers dusting, surface sanitizing, floor care, and bathroom and kitchen detailing. Here's the breakdown our crews work from:

| Area | What's Included |
|---|---|
| Kitchen | Countertops, exterior of appliances, sink, stovetop, backsplash wipe-down, floors mopped, trash emptied |
| Bathrooms | Toilets, tubs, showers, sinks, mirrors, countertops, floors mopped and disinfected |
| Bedrooms | Dusting of furniture and surfaces, floors vacuumed or mopped, beds made (linens not changed unless requested), trash emptied |
| Common areas | Dusting of accessible surfaces, baseboards, light fixtures within reach, floors vacuumed and mopped |

### What's the difference between recurring cleaning and a one-time cleaning?

Recurring cleaning is a maintenance-level visit that keeps an already-clean home in consistent condition. A one-time cleaning is typically deeper and more time-intensive, addressing buildup from a home that hasn't had professional attention recently. Recurring visits build on the work from the last visit; a one-time visit starts fresh.

| | Recurring Cleaning | One-Time Cleaning |
|---|---|---|
| Best for | Homes cleaned on a set schedule | First-time visits, special occasions, resets |
| Frequency | Weekly, biweekly, or monthly | Single booked visit |
| Depth | Maintenance-level, builds visit to visit | More thorough, addresses buildup |
| Contract | None required | None required |
| Pricing | Flat-rate per visit, often lower per visit with frequency | Flat-rate per visit |
| Ideal timing | Ongoing upkeep | Before guests, after events, or as a starting point before switching to recurring |

### What's not included in a standard visit?

Standard visits do not cover inside-oven or inside-refrigerator cleaning, wall washing, window washing (interior glass can sometimes be added), laundry, organizing closets, or exterior work like patios and garages. Ask about add-on or specialty services when you request your quote.

## Preparing Your Home for the Cleaning Team

### Do I need to tidy up before the cleaners arrive?

You don't need to deep clean - that's our job. Clearing clutter from floors and countertops helps the crew spend their time actually cleaning rather than organizing. A few minutes of tidying goes a long way toward a more thorough result.

### What should I do with pets during the cleaning?

Secure pets in a comfortable space away from the areas being cleaned, especially if they're anxious around strangers or vacuum noise. Friendly pets that stay calm can often remain loose, but let your coordinator know in advance so the crew can plan around them safely.

### Why does Scottsdale's climate matter for cleaning frequency?

Scottsdale's arid climate and frequent dust events mean surfaces, floors, and air filters accumulate dust noticeably faster than in humid regions. Many local homeowners choose weekly or biweekly service instead of monthly. The [U.S. Environmental Protection Agency notes that dust and particulate matter accumulate quickly indoors in dry, windy regions](https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq), reinforcing why consistent cleaning cadence matters more here than in milder climates.

**5-minute prep list before your cleaner arrives:**

- Clear countertops of dishes, mail, and loose items
- Pick up floor clutter, especially toys, shoes, and cords
- Secure pets in a separate room or crate
- Put away valuables or fragile items you'd rather the crew not handle
- Leave entry instructions (code, key location) somewhere easy to find
