## Types of House Cleaning Services Available in Scottsdale

Not every cleaning job serves the same purpose. Choosing the right service from the start saves money and delivers better results.

### Standard Recurring Cleaning

Standard recurring cleaning keeps homes in steady maintenance mode. It covers the spaces you interact with daily: kitchen counters, stovetops, sinks, bathroom fixtures, mirrors, floors, and surfaces throughout.

Most Scottsdale clients schedule this weekly, biweekly, or monthly. Biweekly works best for most households, weekly if you have young children or pets, monthly if the home sees lighter use. Typical time windows:

- 1 BR / 1 BA: 2 to 3 hours
- 2 BR / 2 BA: 2.5 to 3.5 hours
- 3 BR / 2 BA: 3.5 to 4.5 hours
- 4+ BR / 3+ BA: 4+ hours

### Deep Cleaning

A deep clean tackles everything in a standard clean plus the neglected zones: baseboards, ceiling fan blades, window tracks, oven interiors, refrigerator interiors, grout lines, behind and under appliances, and cabinet interiors if requested.

Think of it as restoring your home to a professional baseline. Book this for your first cleaning visit, after renovations, at spring start, or after a Scottsdale monsoon season leaves dust in every corner. Deep cleans require more time:

- 1 BR / 1 BA: 3 to 4 hours
- 2 BR / 2 BA: 5 to 5 hours
- 3 BR / 2 BA: 5 to 6 hours
- 4+ BR / 3+ BA: 6+ hours

### Move-In Cleaning

Moving into a new Scottsdale home means inheriting whatever the previous occupants left behind, and in the desert, that often includes substantial particulate dust that settles into corners, light fixtures, and HVAC vents during the time a home sits empty. A move-in clean is essentially a deep clean with extra attention to transition-period problem areas. Cabinet interiors, appliance interiors, closet shelving, and bathroom fixtures all get addressed before you bring your belongings in.

### Move-Out Cleaning

Move-out cleaning in Scottsdale centers on one goal: leaving the property in condition that satisfies landlords, property managers, and real estate agents, while protecting your security deposit. Expect thorough cleaning of all rooms, appliance interiors, cabinet interiors, window sills, baseboards, and any areas specified in your lease's cleaning addendum. This is not the time to cut corners, and professional move-out cleaning almost always pays for itself by protecting your deposit.

### Airbnb and Short-Term Rental Turnover Cleaning

Scottsdale hosts one of [the most active short-term rental markets](https://www.maricopa.gov) in Maricopa County, driven by events like Barrett-Jackson, spring training, and the Waste Management Phoenix Open. Airbnb turnover cleaning operates on a tight timeline between guest checkout and check-in. It includes everything in a standard clean, plus linen handling and bed-making, restocking consumables (soap, toilet paper, paper towels), spot-checking for damage, and ensuring guest-ready presentation standards throughout. Speed and thoroughness must coexist, and a reliable local partner makes that possible.

> **Not sure which service fits?** If your home has never been professionally cleaned or hasn't been cleaned in over a month, start with a deep clean. For regular maintenance, book a recurring standard clean. Moving in or out? Book the corresponding specialty service. Hosting on Airbnb? You need turnover cleaning.

## What's Included (and What's Not) in a Standard House Clean

This is one of the most common sources of confusion when people hire a cleaner for the first time.

### Room-by-Room Standard Cleaning Checklist

**Kitchen**

- Wipe down all counter surfaces
- Clean stovetop exterior and control panels
- Wipe appliance exteriors (microwave, dishwasher, refrigerator front)
- Scrub and polish the sink
- Wipe cabinet fronts
- Sweep and mop the floor
- Empty trash

**Bathrooms**

- Scrub toilet inside and out
- Clean and disinfect sink and counter
- Clean mirror
- Scrub tub and/or shower walls and floor
- Wipe fixtures and towel bars
- Sweep and mop the floor
- Empty trash

**Bedrooms**

- Dust all surfaces including nightstands, dressers, and shelving
- Vacuum or mop floors
- Change and make the bed (if fresh linens are provided)

**Living and Common Areas**

- Dust all surfaces, lamps, and decor items
- Vacuum upholstered furniture
- Vacuum and/or mop all floor surfaces
- Wipe interior window sills
- Empty trash

### What's Typically NOT Included in a Standard Clean

- Inside the oven or refrigerator
- Inside cabinets and drawers
- Laundry washing or folding
- Washing dishes
- Garage, patio, or exterior areas
- Biohazard situations or extreme clutter
- Exterior windows

### Deep Clean Add-Ons Worth Requesting in Scottsdale

Given the desert environment, these extras deliver real value for Scottsdale homes:

- **Ceiling fan blades** - Fans run constantly in Arizona summers and accumulate dust quickly.
- **Blinds and shutters** - Dust builds up quickly here because of their proximity to the windows.
- **Window sills** - Fine desert dust packs into these channels between standard cleanings.
- **Baseboards** - Ground-level dust in a desert climate is relentless.
- **Door trim and interior doors** - Heavily trafficked areas always accumulate debris the fastest.
- **Air return vent covers** - Not always offered or reachable, but worth requesting at least quarterly.

## Scottsdale's Desert Climate and Why It Changes Your Cleaning Needs

Scottsdale homes get dirty faster than most other parts of the country. The [Sonoran Desert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonoran_Desert) surrounding the Phoenix metro produces fine particulate dust that moves through the smallest gaps in windows, doors, and HVAC systems. Add haboobs and monsoon storms that roll through from June through September, and homes near desert preserves or open lots can go from clean to dusty in days.

Here's what that means practically:

- **Biweekly cleaning is more effective than monthly** for most Scottsdale households. The dust cycle simply moves too fast for once-a-month maintenance to keep pace.
- **Ceiling fans and air returns need attention at every visit** - not just during deep cleans - because they recirculate airborne particles constantly.
- **Hard flooring shows desert soil quickly.** Light-colored tile and wood floors common in Scottsdale reveal tracked-in sand and dust after a single outdoor trip.
- **Book a deep clean after monsoon season ends** (typically late September or October) to clear accumulated debris from storm events.
- **Change HVAC filters more often.** The [U.S. Environmental Protection Agency](https://www.epa.gov/saferchoice) emphasizes indoor air quality, and Arizona's dust load clogs filters faster than national averages.

> **Pro tip from Scottsdale cleaners:** If your home sits near a desert preserve, a wash, or an open undeveloped lot, plan for more frequent dusting - especially on ceiling fans, window sills, and hard-surface floors. Seasonal deep cleans before and after monsoon season are worth the investment.

## Is It Safe to Have a Cleaning Team in My Home?

### Background Checks and What They Actually Cover

A thorough background check screens for criminal history, identity verification, and in some cases, driving records for team members who operate company vehicles. This is non-negotiable for any company you invite into your home. At Sun Valley Cleaners, every team member completes a verified background check before their first shift - not a self-reported form, but a third-party verified process.

Insurance and bonding protect you specifically: if a cleaner accidentally breaks a valuable item or if something goes missing, a bonded and insured company can process a claim and make you whole. Without bonding, your only recourse with an uninsured cleaner is small claims court.

### Eco-Friendly and Family-Safe Products

For families with children and pets in Scottsdale, the products your cleaning company uses matter as much as how they clean. Standard commercial cleaning products often contain volatile organic compounds (VOCs) and harsh chemical agents that linger on surfaces and in the air long after the team leaves.

The [EPA's Safer Choice program](https://www.epa.gov/saferchoice) certifies products that meet rigorous safety standards for human health and environmental impact. Look for companies that use Safer Choice-certified or equivalent non-toxic, biodegradable cleaning agents. When you book, ask specifically about fragrance sensitivity and whether the company can accommodate requests for unscented or allergy-friendly products.

For your pets specifically: before the team arrives, move food and water bowls out of areas being cleaned, and keep pets in a separate room or outside during the visit. Most professional-grade products are safe once surfaces dry, but direct contact with wet cleaning agents isn't ideal for curious animals.

### Protecting Valuables and Sensitive Items

Before any cleaning visit:

- Move jewelry, cash, prescription medications, and irreplaceable items to a drawer or room you're securing
- Let your cleaning team know about fragile items that need careful handling
- Establish clear communication about any off-limits areas

A professional team should ask - not assume - about special handling requests. If they don't ask, you should tell them.
