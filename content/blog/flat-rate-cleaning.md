## What Flat-Rate House Cleaning Actually Is

Flat-rate house cleaning quotes a single, fixed price for a specific cleaning job. The price is based on factors like your home's square footage, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, and the type of service you need - whether standard, deep, move-out, or recurring. You know the total cost before work begins, and that price holds firm even if the job runs 20 minutes longer than expected.

This model emerged to solve a real problem with hourly billing: it rewards slow work and penalizes efficient teams, leaving homeowners unable to budget with confidence. Flat-rate pricing reverses that dynamic. The company bears the risk if a job runs long, and you get a number to plan around, whether you're booking a one-time deep clean before guests arrive or scheduling recurring service every other week.

## Flat-Rate vs. Hourly Cleaning: The Core Difference

The distinction matters: hourly pricing charges you for time, while flat-rate pricing charges you for completed work. That single shift changes nearly everything about the experience.

![Side-by-side comparison of flat-rate and hourly cleaning models showing differences in billing basis, predictability, and surprise costs.](https://blogr.ams3.digitaloceanspaces.com/article-visuals/328/flat-rate-vs-hourly-cleaning-pricing-wfwoxe-1.webp)

### Hourly Pricing: How It Works and What Goes Wrong

Under an hourly model, the cleaning company charges a set rate per cleaner per hour, and your final bill reflects how long the job takes. Two cleaners working three hours at $50/hour costs more than the same crew working two hours, even if the home and work performed are identical.

The downsides appear quickly:

- Your final cost remains unknown until the job finishes.
- The crew has little incentive to work quickly - more hours means more revenue for the company.
- Estimating hours for an unfamiliar home is guesswork, which is how homeowners end up with surprise charges.
- Comparing quotes between companies is difficult; "per hour" tells you nothing about how many hours your home actually needs.

### Flat-Rate Pricing: How It Works and What It Offers

With flat-rate pricing, the company evaluates your home's size, layout, and desired service type, then quotes one price covering the entire job. This works better for most homeowners because:

- You know your total cost before booking.
- The cleaning team is motivated to work efficiently rather than stretch the clock.
- Recurring cleaning becomes simple with a consistent monthly cost.
- You're paying for a result, not a time increment, eliminating pressure to rush through rooms.

| Criteria | Flat-Rate Cleaning | Hourly Cleaning |
|---|---|---|
| Billing basis | Fixed price for defined scope of work | Price multiplied by hours worked |
| Predictability | Known total cost before the visit | Final cost unknown until job ends |
| Surprise costs | Low; price locked in upfront | Higher; costs climb if job runs long |
| Crew incentive | Work efficiently and thoroughly | No built-in incentive to finish quickly |
| Best for | Homeowners wanting budget certainty and recurring service | Small, highly unpredictable one-off tasks |

## What Sets Your Flat Rate: Two Main Factors

Every quote depends on how big and complex your home is, and what kind of cleaning you need.

![Checklist of six factors that shape a flat-rate cleaning quote: square footage, bedrooms and bathrooms, service type, home condition, pets, and cleaning frequency.](https://blogr.ams3.digitaloceanspaces.com/article-visuals/328/what-determines-your-flat-rate-quote-wfwoxe-2.webp)

### Square Footage and Layout

Larger homes have more surface area to clean, more floors to vacuum or mop, and more windows and baseboards to address. Layout matters too. An open-concept home can sometimes clean faster than a similarly sized home with multiple small, separated rooms, each requiring its own attention.

### Bedrooms and Bathrooms

Bathrooms tend to drive time and effort most significantly in any cleaning visit. Grout, tile, fixtures, and Arizona's hard water deposits on glass shower doors and faucets all require focused attention. Bedrooms are generally quicker, though homes with bedrooms used for storage or home offices add scope. Two Scottsdale homes of identical square footage can still land at different flat rates based on their bed-and-bath configuration.

### Service Type: Standard, Deep, Recurring, Move-In/Move-Out, and Airbnb Turnover

The cleaning category you choose reshapes the scope significantly:

- **Standard cleaning** covers routine upkeep: dusting, vacuuming, mopping, kitchen and bathroom wipe-downs, and general tidying for a well-maintained home.
- **Deep cleaning** targets baseboards, inside appliances, window tracks, light fixtures, and accumulated buildup between regular visits. It takes longer and costs accordingly.
- **Recurring cleaning** (weekly, biweekly, or monthly) often carries a more favorable flat rate per visit since the home stays maintained and each visit requires less intensive work than a one-time deep clean.
- **Move-in/move-out cleaning** addresses an empty home, reaching every surface, cabinet, closet, and corner without furniture in the way. Previous occupants may have left months of buildup behind.
- **Airbnb turnover cleaning** prioritizes speed and consistency between guest stays using a checklist-driven approach to keep the home guest-ready every time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is flat-rate house cleaning?**
Flat-rate house cleaning is a pricing model where you pay one fixed price for a defined cleaning job, based on your home's size and the service type, rather than paying by the hour.

**How is a flat rate different from an hourly cleaning rate?**
A flat rate is set before the job starts and doesn't change based on how long the visit takes. An hourly rate is billed per hour worked, so your total cost depends on the crew's speed and your home's complexity, and remains uncertain until the job wraps up.

**What factors determine my flat-rate cleaning price?**
Your price is shaped mainly by square footage, the number of bedrooms and bathrooms, and the service type you choose, such as standard, deep, recurring, or move-out cleaning. Home condition and extra requests also play a role.

**Does a bigger home always cost more with flat-rate pricing?**
Generally yes, since more square footage and more bedrooms and bathrooms mean more surface area and fixtures to clean. Layout and condition can shift this somewhat, so two homes of the same size can still land at slightly different rates.

**Does flat-rate pricing include deep cleaning and move-out cleaning?**
Yes. Deep cleaning and move-in/move-out cleaning are both quoted as flat rates, just at a different scope and price point than a standard cleaning, since they involve more detailed work like inside-appliance cleaning and buildup removal.

**How do I get an accurate flat-rate quote in Scottsdale?**
Share your home's square footage, bedroom and bathroom count, and desired service type with the cleaning company. The more specific you are, the more accurate the flat rate will be.

**Can my flat rate change over time?**
Your rate for a given scope of work stays consistent as long as your home's size and service needs remain the same. If you switch from standard to deep cleaning, add square footage through a renovation, or change your cleaning frequency, the rate adjusts to reflect that new scope.

**Is flat-rate pricing better for recurring cleaning service?**
Yes, in most cases. Recurring flat-rate pricing gives you a consistent number to budget around every week, biweekly, or monthly, since the home stays maintained and each visit covers a predictable scope.
